Harmony Studio · v1.0

Your music notebook

  1. Song Atlas follows a complete piece: sections, returns, transitions, harmony and melody listening notes.
  2. Phrase Lab lets you change chords, compare versions, and keep alternatives. Arrange entries with the up/down buttons.
  3. From a song section, choose bars and Explore phrase in Harmony Lab. The phrase keeps its source connection.
  4. From that phrase, choose Try this version in the whole song to save a separate song variation.
  5. Back up everything saves both workspaces in one JSON file. Import it on another computer or in the GitHub Pages version.

You can also import earlier Lab or Atlas backups; each updates its corresponding workspace. Moving the app or switching browsers can change which browser storage is available. Keep your backup with your music files.

The app runs by opening index.html. GitHub Pages can host the same file. No account or server is needed to use the downloaded app. Hosting the program does not sync your personal collection.

The starter song analysis is an audio-informed working draft. Chord uncertainty and melody listening prompts remain visible; loading a recording is playback, rather than automatic transcription.
