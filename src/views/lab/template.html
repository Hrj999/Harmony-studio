      Add a harmony entry

      Each | starts a new 4-beat bar. Chords in a bar share its beats. To set lengths, write Fmaj7:2 F7:2. Supported colours include maj7, m7, 7, 6, m6, add9, maj9, m9, 9, 13, sus4, 7sus4, dim, dim7, m7b5, and aug.

      A laboratory for your ears

      1. Choose a mood or search for a chord or technique. Use Arrange entries above the list to move any entry up or down. The order saves automatically and travels with your library backup.
      2. Play the pattern, or select one chord to hear its exact voicing.
      3. In Your harmony laboratory, type a new chord such as F, Fm7, F6/9, or G7/B. Apply it to one occurrence or a group of matching chords.
      4. Compare Hear original pattern and Hear experiment. You can also rewrite the full phrase to add, remove, or reorder chords. Key and tempo stay under your control.
      5. Give an experiment a name and Save as new dictionary entry. Your original entry remains available. Unsaved experiments also stay in this browser and are included in backups.
      6. Add Real music examples: a song or work, its artist, a listening link, a section or timestamp, and what the melody does over the chords. Examples stay with their entry and saved alternatives.
      7. Use the collection backup in the Studio header after making changes. One backup includes your songs, entries in your chosen order, experiments, notes, real music examples, and favourites. Keep a copy with your music projects.

      Open Harmony Studio in a normal browser whenever you need it. Playback is synthesized locally; it does not require internet or a music-software license. Browser notes may not follow you to a different browser, computer, or copied file, so use backup and restore.

      For Cubase: use File → Import → MIDI File, choose a MIDI export, and assign a piano or another instrument to its tracks. Chords and bass are separate. To explore a Chord Track, select both bass and upper-voice MIDI parts when creating chord events, so the bass is included. Inferred names may differ from the dictionary’s analytical spelling.

      For MuseScore or Sibelius: open the MusicXML export. These are original harmony studies, with exact programmed notes rather than an audio transcription.
